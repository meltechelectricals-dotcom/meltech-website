How cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit the MELTECH website.

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy describes how cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit the MELTECH Electricals and Refrigeration Services website. We aim to explain what these technologies do and the choices available to you.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website may store in your browser. Similar technologies can include local storage, session storage and tools used by embedded or third-party services. They can help a site remember settings, maintain security or understand how a service is used.

2. How this website uses them

The website’s ordinary pages are designed to provide company information, service details and contact options. MELTECH does not intentionally use advertising cookies on these pages. If optional analytics, embedded media, or additional third-party features are introduced, those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies and this policy should be updated to describe them.

3. Third-party services

Some website functions, such as online application submission, may connect to third-party service providers to transmit or store information. Those providers may use technical mechanisms required to deliver or secure their services. Their own policies and settings may also apply to information processed on their platforms.

4. Managing cookies

You can usually view, block or delete cookies through your browser’s privacy or site-settings controls. Blocking all cookies or site storage may affect how some websites or third-party features operate. The exact controls vary by browser and device.

5. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy if the website’s functionality or use of cookies changes. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
