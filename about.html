MELTECH Electricals and Refrigeration Services is a Kenya-based technical services company providing professional solutions across electrical systems, HVAC & refrigeration, clean energy, industrial automation and commercial laundry.

We work with residential, commercial and industrial clients, combining practical engineering expertise, quality workmanship and responsive support to deliver systems that perform efficiently, safely and reliably.

From installation and commissioning to maintenance and repairs, our approach is simple: understand your needs, provide the right technical solution and deliver it with care.
