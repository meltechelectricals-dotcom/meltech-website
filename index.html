ABOUT MELTECH

Engineering solutions built around your needs.

MELTECH Electricals and Refrigeration Services is a Kenya-based technical services company delivering professional electrical, HVAC, refrigeration, clean energy, industrial automation and commercial laundry solutions.

From residential installations to demanding commercial and industrial projects, we combine practical engineering expertise, quality workmanship and responsive service to deliver systems that perform efficiently, safely and reliably.

We believe every project deserves clear communication, professional execution and dependable after-sales support. Our approach is simple: understand your needs, provide the right technical solution, and deliver it with care from installation through ongoing maintenance and support.
